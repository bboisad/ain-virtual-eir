Cedar Pilot Plan
Campus Loop | AIN Virtual EIR demonstration

This is a fictional planning document for prototype testing.

Pilot: Cedar
Owner: Alice
Scheduled date: November 20, 2026
Budget: $36
Recruitment target: 10 students
Test price: $3 per prepaid listing
Status: Planned. No results have been collected.

Purpose
Test whether students are willing to prepay a listing fee before using
Campus Loop to sell items.

Planned observations
Record the number who actually prepay and the reasons given by students
who decline. The existing demonstration experiment is supported if at
least 4 of the 10 participants prepay $3; fewer than 4 refutes it.

This plan describes intended work. It does not report completed research,
actual payments, or verified customer evidence.
