# Campus Loop: move-out interview notes (Bob, Oct 5)

Fictional demo data. Three students who moved out of residence halls last May, 15 minutes each.

## Interview 1: junior, moved out of a double room
- Left a mini-fridge and a desk lamp in the hallway. "Carrying the fridge down four floors was not happening."
- Would use a pickup or resale service "if it's free". Would not pay to list: "I'm already losing money on it."

## Interview 2: senior, moved out of a single room
- Sold a futon to a friend for $20; left a rug and a shelf behind.
- Said a $3 listing fee "sounds fine for something worth more than $20", but has never paid to sell anything.

## Interview 3: sophomore, moved out of a triple room
- Left three items in the hallway (chair, mirror, storage bins). Did not know items left in rooms are fined.
- "Only if it costs me nothing. Maybe the person buying should pay."

## What we heard
- 3 of 3 had bulky items they did not take home.
- 2 of 3 would only use the service if it is free for sellers.
- 1 of 3 said a $3 fee is fine for items worth more than $20.
- Nobody has paid for anything yet: everything above is what people said.
